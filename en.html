Konya in brief

Independent city guide — not an official tourism office.

Why Konya?

Former capital of the Anatolian Seljuks and the city of Rumi (Mevlana). Come for Sufi heritage, museums, and hearty local food.

One-day outline

  • Morning: Mevlana Museum (go early)
  • Lunch: etli ekmek (thin flatbread with minced meat) + ayran
  • Afternoon: Alaaddin Hill + nearby madrasas/museums
  • Evening: Meram or a park stroll
Printable planMap

Food to try

  • Etli ekmek
  • Fırın kebabı
  • Tirit, bamya soup, arabaşı (seasonal)
  • Hoşmerim / cezerye for sweets

Day trips

  • Sille — village atmosphere near the city
  • Beyşehir — lake & Eşrefoğlu Mosque
  • Akşehir — Nasreddin Hodja heritage
  • Seydişehir — Seyyid Harun complex, cave, park

Practical

  • Emergency: 112
  • Check museum hours on official channels — they change
  • December can be busy (Şeb-i Arus period)
